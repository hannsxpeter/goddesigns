What your team sees after adding one script tag

Every figure in these panels, and in the dashboard above, is sample data from a demo site, example.com.

Install with one tag

Paste the tag into the head of your site. It sets no cookies and writes nothing to the visitor's device. Until the first pageview arrives, the dashboard tells you what to check.

Filter by clicking

Click a page, referrer, country or campaign and every number on the dashboard narrows to it. The filter is part of the URL, so sending the link sends the exact view.

Count trial starts as goals

Call one function from your app when a visitor starts a trial, or reaches any other step you want counted. Each goal shows how many visitors converted and the page they most often entered on.

Campaigns from your UTM tags

Links tagged with utm_source and utm_campaign get their own rows, with visitors and trial starts side by side. There is nothing to set up.

The Monday email

Each teammate can opt in to a plain-text summary at 08:00 in their own time zone, with the week's totals and the source that moved most.
