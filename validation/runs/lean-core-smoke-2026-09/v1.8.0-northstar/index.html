Counted without cookies. Each visitor is counted once a day and cannot be followed from one day to the next. Trials are credited to the page and source where the visit began.

That line is the whole record.

When a visit arrives, its IP address and full browser string are used once, to find the country and tell today’s visitors apart, and then dropped. Nothing is written to the visitor’s device, so there is no cookie to ask permission for.

How visitors are told apart: we hash the IP address, browser string and your domain with a salt that is deleted at midnight UTC. The next day, the same person counts as someone new.

What the record is good for

  • Goals you name. Mark a page or a button as a goal and it gets its own column. Trials started, in the report above, is one; adding another takes one attribute in your HTML.
  • Sources by name. Referrers and campaign tags survive, so the newsletter and the launch post show up by name beside the trials they brought in.
  • The Monday email. The report above arrives at 8:00 on Monday in your time zone, for anyone on the team who wants it. Replies go to the people who build Northstar Signal.
  • Shared links. Give a contractor or an investor a private, read-only link to one site’s dashboard, and switch it off when the work is done.
  • A script you can read. Its source is public, so you can check every claim in this letter against the code. It loads after your page and never sets a cookie. Read the script’s source
